CribbageX Interactive Tutorial — UI mockups

Static mockups for every component in detailed-tutorial-implementation-plan.md. Work-item IDs (U1, T3, S4…) match the plan and the source spec. Nothing here is wired to the engine: totals, groups, and feedback text are hand-written stand-ins for values that must come from scoreHandDetailed, rankDiscards, and explainPegPlay (invariant I1).

Self-contained: no CDN, no external images, no network. Card art is drawn in CSS as a placeholder for deck.getFaceImageUri() PNGs; the text badge beside each card is a real requirement (T3), not a mockup shortcut.

In Learn.tsx under id="rules" (unchanged copy)
Object of the game
Cribbage is a two-player race to 121 points. The first player to peg out — to reach or pass 121 — wins. Points come from the play (pegging), from counting each four-card hand, and from the crib.
Deal and cut for dealer
Each player cuts a card. The low card deals first. After that the deal alternates. The dealer gives six cards to each player.
Scoring reference
CombinationPoints
Each 152
Pair / three of a kind / four of a kind2 / 6 / 12
Run1 per card
Flush in hand (four cards)4
Flush in hand with matching starter5
Crib flush (five cards the same suit only)5
Nobs (jack of the starter's suit)1
His heels (jack as starter)2 to the dealer
How the opponent plays
  • Easy — plays a reasonable card without looking ahead.
  • Intermediate — counts the obvious points.
  • Expert — maximises expected value on every discard.

The same shuffle at every difficulty — only the opponent's strategy changes.
